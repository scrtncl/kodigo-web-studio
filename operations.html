Section 6  ·  Operational Procedures

Business Operations

How I plan to take a website from the first conversation to launch, while balancing projects with my studies.

Production Workflow

I would agree on the pages, content, price, and delivery schedule before starting.

From first conversation to launch

  1. 1. Discovery & Consultation I start with a short call or email conversation about the business, its customers, services, preferred style, and contact options.
  2. 2. Architecture & Content Agreement I sketch the pages and agree on the text, photos, and colors with the client before building.
  3. 3. Hand-Coded Development The site is coded from scratch with clean HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, and Bootstrap 5 components. I plan to check desktop and mobile layouts with browser tools and available devices, documenting any testing limits.
  4. 4. Client Staging Review I share a preview link for review; access protection depends on the selected hosting service. The client tests the buttons, reviews copy, and requests refinements (one complete revision cycle included).
  5. 5. Deployment & Handover Upon approval, the site is deployed to free, high-speed CDN infrastructure (GitHub Pages or Netlify). I connect a client-owned domain where included, hand over the code, and provide a short guide to updates.

Operating Hours & Communication

Proposed availability: Monday to Saturday, around my class schedule.
I aim to reply by email within two working days. Project deadlines are agreed individually; emergency support is not included.

Payment & Milestone Terms

  • 50% Initial Deposit: Due after the scope and schedule are agreed.
  • 50% Final Payment: Released only after the client approves the live staging URL.
  • Payment Channels: To be agreed with the client before payment; no payment account is published on this proposal.
  • Post-Launch Warranty: 7 days for Starter and Business; 14 days for Premium. New features are quoted separately.
