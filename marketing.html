Section 5  ·  Go-To-Market Plan

Marketing Strategy

A cost-effective, trust-first client acquisition plan built on genuine community proof, hyper-local digital promotion, and transparent value pricing.

How I’ll find my first clients

A proposed launch plan built around local conversations, useful examples, and referrals.

1. Organic Promotion & Social Proof
Maintain an active Facebook and Instagram presence showcasing side-by-side website transformations, live screen previews, and quick actionable advice (e.g., "3 reasons why keeping your menu in a Facebook photo post loses you customers"). This establishes technical credibility without aggressive sales pressure.
2. Targeted Local Advertising
Allocate a lean ad budget of ₱300/month as an initial advertising allowance, with targeting to be tested for local business owners located within a 5–10 km radius. Combine this with physical QR-code business cards distributed at local print shops, internet cafes, and neighborhood cooperatives.
3. Penetration Pricing & Incentives
Offer a proposed 30% launch discount to the first five clients. Ask for honest feedback and separate permission before sharing their work. A 10% referral reward would be based on the website fee actually collected after discounts, paid only after full payment. The financial calculator models both costs.
4. Client Retention & Support
Share a preview for review before the final 50% payment. Include 7 days of bug fixes for Starter and Business, or 14 days for Premium. Offer optional content updates at ₱500/month for up to one hour of work.

My proposed 90-day launch plan

  1. Phase 1: Weeks 1–2 (Foundation & Branding) Deploy the official studio site on GitHub Pages / Netlify. Set up official Facebook and Instagram pages. Publish 3 clearly labeled sample website layouts and circulate them through academic and alumni networks.
  2. Phase 2: Weeks 3–6 (Early Adopter Outreach) Pitch 10 friendly local store owners (bakeries, salons, tutorial centers) with the 30% founding client discount. Secure the first 3 paying clients and document the complete design cycle.
  3. Phase 3: Weeks 7–10 (Testimonial & Case Study Packaging) Ask early clients what worked and what was confusing. With permission, publish screenshots and honest feedback. Only include time savings or inquiry results if they were actually measured.
  4. Phase 4: Weeks 11–13 (Scale & Paid Amplification) Review which outreach brought inquiries and decide whether to continue the small ad trial. Roll out the 10% customer referral program and offer monthly maintenance packages.
