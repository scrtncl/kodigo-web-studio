Before we build

A few things you might be wondering.

Clear expectations, from your first inquiry to the handover.

What do I need to provide before we start?

Your business name, logo if available, approved text, photos you have permission to use, contact details, and the pages or features you need. We agree on the scope and schedule before the initial deposit.

When will my website be ready?

The proposed estimates are 5 business days for Starter, 10 for Business, and 14 for Premium. The timeline begins after the deposit and all content are received, and depends on my class schedule and the agreed scope.

How do revisions and payments work?

Each package includes one complete revision round. Proposed payment terms are 50% after agreeing on the scope and schedule, and 50% after you approve the staging website. Additional requests outside the agreed scope need a separate quote.

Are a domain and hosting included in the price?

Domain registration and renewal are separate costs. Starter includes setup guidance; Business includes configuration; Premium includes domain connection and setup on a suitable free hosting plan. Paid hosting, form services, and backend systems are quoted separately.

What happens after launch?

Starter and Business include 7 days of bug fixes; Premium includes 14 days. New features and content changes are separate from bug fixes. Optional monthly content updates cost ₱500 for up to one hour of text, photo, or price changes.

Can a booking form accept payments or reserve a slot?

The proposed booking feature collects requests. Payment processing and a live reservation system are outside the listed package scope and would need a separate agreement.
