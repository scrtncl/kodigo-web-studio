Section 4  ·  Market Opportunity

Target Market

Identification and detailed analysis of our primary customer segments, their digital pain points, and why conventional agency pricing leaves them underserved.

Customer Segmentation

My proposed customers are small businesses in Porac and nearby communities, plus student organizations. I’m focusing on owners who need a clear menu, service list, or event page alongside their existing social accounts.

Target Group Core Pain Point What Kodigo Delivers
Food & Retail Businesses Prices, flavor menus, and daily specials get buried under weeks of old Facebook posts. Customers leave because they cannot quickly view prices. A fast-loading single-page menu with high-res photos, clear prices, and an instant "Order via Messenger" button.
Local Service Providers Salons, appliance repair technicians, and clinics struggle to establish legitimacy without a formal portfolio or public list of service rates. A trustworthy showcase website featuring service lists, opening hours, customer reviews, and appointment request forms.
Student Orgs & Campus Projects Event registrations and announcements scatter across disparate group chats, resulting in low attendance and lost signups. Clean event registration landing pages, officer directories, and project showcases that look sharp on any smartphone.

Illustrative customer profiles: these are planning examples, not interviewed customers or existing clients. I would validate these needs through conversations with local owners before launch.

Persona 1: "Ate Maria"

Home Baker • Porac & Angeles City (Age 32)

Bakes customized birthday bento cakes and ube pastries. Spends hours every evening answering identical Messenger DMs: "Magkano po yung 6-inch customized cake at delivery to Angeles?".

Kodigo solution: Starter Page featuring an upfront visual menu, flavor guide, and instant Messenger order button.

Persona 2: "Kuya Ben"

Electronics Tech • Porac Town Proper (Age 27)

Operates an independent laptop, tablet, and smartphone repair hub. Relies primarily on walk-ins and loses clients to shopping mall service centers because he lacks a searchable web presence or public rate card.

Kodigo solution: Business Site showcasing repair services, warranty terms, and Google Maps directions.

Persona 3: "AUF Student Org"

College Society • Angeles University Foundation

Organizing an inter-university hackathon or IT symposium. Requires a high-impact promotional landing page to pitch corporate tech sponsors and centralize participant team registration without relying on clunky forms.

Kodigo solution: Interactive event landing page with countdown timer, speaker list, and team registration form.

Why local businesses might choose Kodigo

  • Accessible Price Point: Proposed packages start at ₱3,500. These prices still need to be tested against actual project hours and customer budgets.
  • Zero Monthly Software Lock-in: I plan to use HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Bootstrap with a suitable free hosting plan. Domain renewals and optional paid services would be explained separately.
  • Local Context & Language: I’m based in Porac and want to build around how each customer already takes inquiries, such as Messenger or email.
