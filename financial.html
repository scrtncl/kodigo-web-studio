The 12-month figure repeats this scenario; it is not a forecast. Launch discounts are limited to the first five clients overall and would not recur every month. The surplus excludes owner pay, taxes, depreciation, and any extra client-specific costs. The recovery estimate uses the full startup budget, including the operating reserve.

What I still need to validate

The baseline assumes two Starter projects and one Business project per month. That may be ambitious alongside classes. Before launch, I would track the hours needed for sample builds, confirm setup costs, and ask local owners about their budgets. A quieter month or extra revisions would reduce the surplus. The first-year goal of 10 websites remains a target; this calculator explores different monthly volumes.
